# Provenance and assumptions

## Origin

All records are generated by `src/pipeline.py` with Python's pseudo-random generator and fixed seed `20260923`. No external dataset, confidential document, source-system export, API, account or company system is used. Fictional manufacturers are explicitly prefixed with `Fictional`. Plant names are generic directions.

The portfolio uses no confidential data from Prometheus, Huntsman, Flowserve, Basin Electric, Struer Kommune, Aarhus University, Siemens Gamesa, LEGO or Vestas. No organization sponsors, validates or endorses this case study. Public sources are not needed to reproduce the generated dataset.

## Generation assumptions

- 180 synthetic assets across three plants and four types, with installation dates before 2025.
- 600 initially valid work orders sampled across those assets during calendar year 2025. Some assets have no work orders.
- Corrective-event probability is 48% for compressors and 25% for other types. This deliberately encoded relationship makes the case useful for interpretation exercises; it is not an empirical benchmark.
- Corrective costs are sampled between EUR 600 and 3,500; planned costs between EUR 100 and 750. Corrective durations range from 2 to 24 hours; planned durations from 0.5 to 4 hours. These are illustrative, not market estimates.
- Four extra work orders contain known date, reference, amount or flag defects. Work orders that refer to held master records are also held.
- Four redundant master rows, two conflicting duplicate variants and three missing-key variants are added. Existing records contain formatting, completeness, criticality and date defects.
- Assets are assumed in scope all year. Maintenance dates alone cannot identify overlapping downtime, actual operating exposure or service availability.

## Generated outputs

CSV files use UTF-8, comma separators, one header row and ISO dates. Accepted tables and aggregate tables are ready for SQL clients, Excel or Power BI. The `.sqlite` database is rebuilt locally and not versioned. The SVG is a generated illustration of the results, not a screenshot of a live operational system or Power BI application.

No personal data is included. The synthetic dataset is distributed under the repository's MIT license. No quantified real-world improvement or savings should be inferred from synthetic cleaning rates or spending totals.
